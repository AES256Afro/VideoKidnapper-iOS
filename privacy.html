Privacy Policy

VideoKidnapper for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS
Effective: October 18, 2026

The short version

VideoKidnapper does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections. Nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.

Photos and videos

You choose which videos to edit using the system's media picker, so the app can only access the specific videos you select. To save your finished MP4 or GIF exports, the app asks for permission to add items to your photo library. This add-only access cannot view, modify, or delete anything already in your library.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the effective date above will be revised.
